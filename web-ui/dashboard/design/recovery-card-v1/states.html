{{NAME}} — the recovery card, design states v1

One device's recovery line: the state in three words (verbatim VOCAB: a), since when, what the system last did about it, and the one act that helps. Every pair below shows the same markup in the dark theme (the recorded default) and in light. Shapes and labels carry the state; colour reinforces. The L2 details open on click (a static <details>; no script). The product name appears only as the token in this header — Register C keeps it out of every card sentence (SPEC §12 Q1).
