## §1 What the card is for

The card is one device's recovery line, read by someone who did not install the system: what the device is doing, in three words; since when; what the system last did about it; and the one act that helps. The stranger who must understand it is a household member at 7 a.m. with one plug dark — the kitchen kettle did not come on, the dashboard is open on a phone, and the question is "is it broken, and what do I do". The bar is the stranger test: the person reads the state line aloud and is right about three things — whether the device is talking, whether the system has asked it, and what to do next. Every sentence keeps the honesty law: a value the wire did not carry is never shown as if it had; a null is a plain sentence, never a blank or an invented verb; a device that has not been asked is not "not responding (asked twice)"; a device that answers when asked but sends nothing is "quiet", not fine. The three state words are Nick's (`VOCAB: a`, verbatim); the household never sees `stale`.

## §2 Information architecture

**Where it appears — three surfaces, one component.** (1) The device row in the Devices list: the state line replaces today's "Available / Offline / Not determined yet" cell and the `availabilityEvidence` prose; a dark row carries the act inline. (2) The entity detail page: the card sits under the name, above the attributes; the act and the gesture card live here in full. (3) The hero's why-not card: when the verdict is `NEVER_TRIGGERED` and the wire names the watched entity (`triggerRef`, the "Watching: {entity}" line), and that entity's row is UNAVAILABLE or the fifth state, the recovery card renders under "Watching", so "why didn't it fire" ends in the dark device and its act. The join on every surface is the entity row's `availability`, `lastReported`, `deviceId` (and, after the mirror bump, `availabilityReason`, `lastSeenAt`); the why-not surface joins `triggerRef.id` to that row.

**Two disclosure levels, and no third.** L1 is the state line — the label (one of the five rows of §3, with its instant) and one sentence beneath it (the contract sentence of §4, or the row's own line), then the act when the row is dark. L2 is the technical fact one expand away: the absolute instants (last report; last heard; last asked, with its outcome), the recorded reason token verbatim, the signal at the last frame, the open window's bounds and scope. Nothing else appears: no entity id unless the registry cannot name the device (then the id, verbatim, with the fact that no device is on record), no index paths, no graph, no LQI in L1.

**The one act** is "Open a window for this device" (§5). It never removes a device, never opens a window for every device, never restarts anything.

**What never appears:** `stale` (§6 B); the word "offline" (it claims a fact about the device's power that the record does not hold); a probe count the wire did not carry; a gesture the record does not hold; a competitor; the product name in a card sentence (Register C — §12 Q1).

## §3 The state table

Five rows. Each row is a label with its instant slot, the sentence beneath (the contract sentence of §4 unless the row names its own), the wire fields that decide it, the honest variant when a slot is null, the copy key, and the shape / label / colour role. The three columns are the three stages: **S1** today — the four mirror keys (`availability · stale · deviceId · lastReported`); **S2** after the mirror bump — plus `availabilityReason · lastSeenAt · link`; **S3** after AVAIL-API-1 — plus the FIELDS.md §3 keys (`availabilityClass · reportIntervalSeconds · silenceLimitSeconds · lastProbeOutcome · lastProbeAt · probeMisses`). A sentence may only read fields its column's stage carries; "not sayable" is written out, never left blank, and the row says what the card shows instead.

**"Since {t}"** is always a projection instant: `lastReported` for Reporting and Quiet; `lastSeenAt` for Not responding when present, else `lastReported` — and L2 says which ("last heard" vs "last report"). Never a run row's time. Instants are ISO strings, parsed only through `format.parseInstant`; L1 shows the relative form with the absolute, date-qualified form in L2.

## §4 The contract sentence, per class

The numbers are the record's (the AVAIL-SHAPE instruction §4.5; `MAINS_PING_SILENCE` 60 s; the metering contract 600 s); the words are this spec's, in Register C (no "we", no product name — §12 Q1). Minutes are rounded from seconds for the household (660 s → "11 minutes"); L2 may carry the seconds.

- **The metered mains class** (the Shelly Gen4 plugs; the TR3): `recovery.contract.metered` — "This plug reports at least every 10 minutes. After 11 minutes of silence it is asked." (600 s reporting maximum; limit 660 s; named dark ≈ 670 s after the last report, after two unanswered asks.)
- **The floor mains class** (the S31 Lite; the Hue): `recovery.contract.floor` — "This plug reports at least every minute. After one minute of silence it is asked." (60 s; named dark at ≈ 70 s.) The {kind} slot carries "plug" or "lamp" from the device's class word.
- **The passive class** (the sensor): `recovery.contract.passive` — "This sensor reports when something changes. It is never asked."
- **A hypothetical contract ≤ 60 s** keeps the form — the floor is added, never maxed (U-2): a 30-s contract reads "at least every 30 seconds… after 90 seconds of silence".
- **The class is not on the wire today** (S1, S2): `recovery.contract.fallback` — "If this device goes quiet for a while, it is asked. How long is not shown here yet." The sentence is the honest degraded form, not a placeholder (the hub holds the limit; the read does not carry it, so "not shown here", never "not recorded"); FIELDS.md §3 records `availabilityClass`, `reportIntervalSeconds` and `silenceLimitSeconds` as the missing fields.

## §5 The act — open a window for this device

The act opens J2's device-scoped recovery window: one tap sends `POST /api/v1/integrations/{integrationId}/permit-join` with `durationSeconds` (254, the maximum — §12 Q2), a `reason` ("recovery card: {deviceName}") and `scope` = this device's IEEE (`0x` + 16 hex). The response's `closesAt` drives the countdown; the view's keys are `durationSeconds · opensAt · closesAt · scope` (`PermitJoinEndpoint.java:247–:253`; `scope` is present only when scoped). The window closes by itself at `closesAt`; nothing restarts; no key changes. The moments, each as full copy (§7 keys in brackets):

1. **Before** (the row is R3, R4c or R5): the button `recovery.act.button` "Open a window for this device", with `recovery.act.before` beneath: "A window of about 4 minutes lets this device rejoin. Only this device can join through it." (254 s; "about", because 254 s is 4 min 14 s) When `deviceId` is null: no button; `recovery.act.noDevice` "No device is on record for this entity, so no window can be opened for it."
2. **Open, with the countdown:** `recovery.act.open` "Window open — 3 min 40 s left. Only this device can join." as an info pill with the provisional (dashed) outline; `recovery.act.closing` "The window closes on its own." The countdown is computed from `closesAt` against the client clock on each tick, never from a local timer alone; it renders every second but is announced at 60 s, 30 s and 10 s and at close (§8). Beneath it, the gesture card.
3. **The gesture card, per class** (`recovery.act.gesture.title` "At the device:"): the record holds a gesture for two classes and none for the rest, and no gesture is invented. The SNZB-06P24 presence sensor class: `recovery.act.gesture.sensorListens` "Nothing to press. After losing power, this sensor looks for an open window on its own." (IR-117, D-v98-3: joined 15.9 s after the window opened, on the window alone; a button hold on this class makes it leave — IR-114 — and is never offered.) The Hue lamp class: `recovery.act.gesture.lampResets` "This lamp rejoins after a reset. Its reset steps are not on record yet." (the bench's device note `corpus/devices/philips-hue-white-a19.md:16` names a power-cycle sequence or the vendor app without the steps.) Every other class today — the Shelly Gen4 plugs, the TR3, the S31 Lite — `recovery.act.gesture.none` "The steps for this device are not on record yet." §12 Q7 lists them. The "bring it close" cue is a cue, not a rule (target 2 is CONTESTED — the intake audit §2): `recovery.act.cue.close` "If nothing happens, try again with the device nearer the hub." appears after the gesture line, never before it.
4. **A foreign joiner during the window:** `recovery.act.rejected` "Another device tried to join and was turned away. The window is still open for this device — 2 min 10 s left." — a calm neutral line under the countdown, never an alarm. Today it has no read (`join_rejected` lands in the store; B1 `/api/v1/events` is FROZEN-UNBUILT), so the line ships behind B1's presence; the spec shows it so the shape is settled.
5. **Rejoined:** the row flips to R1 by its own frame (`first_contact` / `frame_received`); the card shows `recovery.act.rejoined` "It is back. Reporting since 7:21 am." in the ok register and the countdown pill is replaced by `recovery.act.closing` until `closesAt`. This moment is readable TODAY from the entity row alone.
6. **Closed by itself, nothing rejoined:** `recovery.act.closedNothing` "The window closed and this device did not join. Check that it has power, then open a window again." — the remedy first (physical-first: the record's dark spans were cables and power), the button again beneath. Never a silent blank: every moment shows progress, a next action, or a remedy.
7. **The request failed:** one honest, named, retryable error card — `recovery.act.error.title` "The window could not be opened." with the cause by status: 503 `recovery.act.error.notRunning` "The radio is not running right now."; 409 `recovery.act.error.busy` "A window is already open. Wait for it to close, then try again."; anything else `recovery.act.error.generic` "The request did not go through. Nothing was changed."; the button `recovery.act.error.retry` "Try again". 401 is the auth gate's, not the card's.

**Each surface, twice.** TODAY the window's view exists only in the POST's response: the countdown lives in the component that opened it, and a reload loses it (the row then reads its state honestly, without "window open"); the device row and the why-not card show the act's button and, after a tap, the countdown; the gesture card falls to "no steps yet" for every class but the two above. ONCE THE KEYS LAND (FIELDS.md §3): the countdown survives a reload through the open-window read; the gesture card picks its class from `availabilityClass` and the device's model; the foreign-joiner line reads from B1.

**What the card does not do:** it never removes a device; it never opens an unscoped window; it never opens a window from the list for every dark device at once; it never implies the window delivered anything (a rejoin is proved only by the row's own frame).

## §6 The two notes

**§A → `FIELDS.md`.** The fields the card reads are the row of record in `FIELDS.md` (three tables: IN-MIRROR, ON-WIRE-NOT-IN-MIRROR, NOT-ON-WIRE). The hub cuts AVAIL-API-1 from that file alone; this spec names no field FIELDS.md omits.

**§B The word per surface — IR-134 closed by vocabulary.** Two freshness models exist on one row: `stale` (computed at read time from `staleAfter`, the staleness chain of Doc 03 §3.8 — a reading's freshness) and `availability` (J1's probe clock — the device's reachability). They disagree by construction (IR-134: `stale=false` beside `UNAVAILABLE ping_timeout` through a whole dark window), so one word per thing: **the recovery card never shows `stale`**; it shows the five rows above, about the device. `stale` keeps showing where it is about a reading: the attribute values on the entity detail (today's "Stale reading" pill beside the values, `DevicesView.tsx:152`) and the metering reads; the device row's "Stale" pill (`DevicesView.tsx:90`) moves off the row and onto the value cell it describes. The hero's "As of {time} · live" line is poll freshness and keeps its words. The build row for this is §11 row 2.

## §8 Accessibility

Contrast was computed from the generated `tokens.css` values at `da9ca3d` (WCAG 2.x relative luminance; the pill text is 11–12 px, so 4.5:1 applies to every state pair). Light: ok 5.43 / 6.16; warn 5.84 / 6.56; error 5.28 / 6.14; info 6.04 / 6.79; unknown 5.17 / 6.04; body 17.46; secondary 6.79 (5.89 on sunk); muted 5.85 (5.49 on the page background — hero-v1's T1 is applied in light). The light focus ring reads 4.76 on surface (above the 3:1 non-text bar).

**Shape and label on every state**, colour reinforcing: R1 the pulse (a report arriving); R2 two opposed arrows (asked, answered); R3 two outgoing arrows, nothing returning (asked twice, no answer); R4 the listening dot (reporting) and the dotted clock (its interval passed, never asked); R5 a question mark (not asked, not known); the open vocabulary the dotted line. R5's shape is distinct from R3's by construction — a question mark beside two arrows — and the labels differ in their verb ("not asked" vs "no answer"); §10 checks both with the colour covered. Each glyph is `aria-hidden`; the card carries `recovery.a11y.state` as visually-hidden text.

**The countdown** renders every second but announces through one polite `role="status"` region at 60 s, 30 s, 10 s and at close (`recovery.a11y.countdown`, `recovery.a11y.closed`) — never every second. **Reduced motion:** under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the pill swaps state without a fade and the countdown has no progress animation (the digits change; nothing moves). **Focus order** follows reading order: the state label (not a stop) → the L2 `<summary>` (`recovery.a11y.expand`) → the act's button → when open, the gesture card's `<summary>` → the error card's retry. The `<summary>` and the buttons are the only stops; the countdown pill is not a stop. Sentences stay ≤ 16 words (§7 measures each).

## §10 The acceptance script

Six sentences a stranger reads aloud from the mockups, and what "right" means. The states — (1) "Kitchen Plug — Reporting. Last report 2 minutes ago. This plug reports at least every 10 minutes; after 11 minutes of silence it is asked." Right means the person knows the plug is talking and that nothing needs doing. (2) "Kitchen Plug — Not responding since 6:52 am (asked twice, no answer). Asked twice; nothing came back." Right means the person knows the system tried, twice, and heard nothing, and sees the one thing to do. The act — (3) "Window open — 3 min 40 s left. Only this device can join. At the device: nothing to press. After losing power, this sensor looks for an open window on its own." Right means the person waits, does not press anything, and knows no other device can join. (4) "The window closed and this device did not join. Check that it has power, then open a window again." Right means the person looks at the plug's socket before tapping again, and is not alarmed. The fifth state and the passive class — (5) "Porch Lamp — Not heard from since startup (not asked). Nothing from this device since startup. It has not been asked yet." Right means the person understands the system has NOT tried yet, which is a different fact from "asked twice, no answer", and can say so. (6) "Hallway Sensor — Not responding since 11:19 am. Nothing has arrived for longer than this device usually goes. It is never asked." Right means the person knows the sensor is dark and that "never asked" is how this kind of device works, not a fault. A seventh check runs on every mockup: the person points at any coloured marker and names its state from the label and shape with the colour covered — and, with R3 and R5 side by side, says which one the system asked.

## §12 Open questions for Nick

Q1 — **Register C vs the charter's wording.** The DAS (§1.1, §1.3) forbids self-reference in UI copy — neither the product name nor "we" — and hero-v1's Q3 recommended the name-free footer on the same ground. The charter's draft sentences carry both ("we ask after 11"; "{{NAME}} has not heard…"). Options: (a) Register C throughout — "After 11 minutes of silence it is asked." / "Nothing from this device since startup. It has not been asked yet." (this spec; `{{NAME}}` appears in no card string); (b) the charter's literal "we" form, as a sanctioned household register for this one card; (c) `{{NAME}}` as the subject ("{{NAME}} asks after 11 minutes"), one brand moment per card. Recommendation (a): rename-proof, consistent with the hero, and the verb still names who acted (the passive voice says the system did it without naming it). Does not block the build — the keys are the same either way.

Q2 — **The window the card opens.** Options: (a) 254 s, the maximum the endpoint allows (this spec: the field's countdown is 254 s where it exists; the SNZB class joined in 15.9 s but a sleepy class may take longer); (b) 120 s with a "keep it open" tap; (c) 60 s. Recommendation (a). Does not block.

Q3 — **The dashboard's first write.** The client is GET-only (`client.ts:22`); the act is the first POST the dashboard makes, behind the same token the reads use. Options: (a) add `post` to the client and ship the act in the same build as the card (this spec, §11 row 3); (b) ship the card read-only first and the act as its own unit after a review of the write path (the token in the request, the readiness gate, 409 on a busy integration); (c) the act stays an operator's verb (`bench.sh permit-join`) for the cohort and the household sees the gesture card only. Recommendation (b): the card's value is mostly in its words, and a write deserves its own review. Blocks §11 row 3 only.

Q4 — **The passive class's "Quiet".** No number on the record separates a quiet sensor from a reporting one: the sensor declares no interval (IR-121; the 25-h window), so at every stage the passive Quiet row is "not sayable" and the card shows Reporting with the age. Options: (a) Quiet only when a declared interval exists (`reportIntervalSeconds` present) — this spec; (b) a fixed household cadence (24 h) as the threshold — a hypothesis, not a measurement; (c) declare the interval per class in the capability registry (the J3-class unit IR-121 names) and let (a) follow. Recommendation (a) now, (c) when the soak's sensor row lands. Does not block.

Q5 — **The restart ambiguity.** A device named dark before a restart (`ping_timeout`, `lastSeenAt` on record) is seeded UNAVAILABLE and never asked again in the new process (`:465–:467`), yet the wire keeps the old reason. Options: (a) at S2 the label stands on the recorded transition — "(asked twice, no answer)" — and S3's `lastProbeAt` null flips it to R5's words (this spec); (b) at S2 treat any UNAVAILABLE device as R5 unless `lastSeenAt` is younger than the hub's own start — the hub's start is not on the wire, so (b) needs `meta` to carry it; (c) a tracker change (probe a seeded-dark mains device once at boot) — Java, the hub's call, outside this lane. Recommendation (a), with (c) raised to the hub as an observation: the never-asked class exists because of the skip, and one probe at boot would retire R5 on silicon for mains devices. Does not block.

Q6 — **Scoping the window by IEEE or by device.** The POST takes `scope` as an IEEE; the entity row carries a `deviceId` (a ULID). Options: (a) `ieeeAddress` as an additive row key (FIELDS.md §3; the hub's unit); (b) the POST accepts `deviceId` and resolves the IEEE server-side (a change to PJ-2's body, a second one-way door); (c) both. Recommendation (a): additive, read-side, no change to the command surface. Blocks §11 row 3 only.

Q7 — **The gesture cards with no steps.** The record holds a recovery gesture for the SNZB-06P24 class (none needed) and a fact without steps for the Hue; nothing for the Shelly Gen4 plugs, the TR3 or the S31 Lite — the card says so. Options: (a) ship "no steps yet" and let the cohort's observation 2 (gesture count and kind) fill the rows; (b) a bench card per class that records the reset gesture from the manual and one try, before the card ships; (c) transcribe the manuals without a try. Recommendation (a) for the build and (b) for the record — no gesture is invented. Does not block.

Q8 — **"Not responding" in the error register.** Options: (a) error (red) for R3 and R4's dark row — the system asked twice (or the interval passed) and the fact is known, and the household can act (this spec); (b) warn (amber) for both, keeping red for the act's failure only; (c) error for R3, warn for R4. Recommendation (a): the hue is the honest severity of a known-dark device, and the fifth state's purple keeps "not asked" visibly apart from it. Does not block.
